A one-day world news front page for **Saturday 21 November 2026**, built on the
layout the leaderboard's top entries share: the date itself is the masthead,
a six-number strip and a region-count bar sit under it, and the page carries
3 heroes + 10 mid stories, every one with its own picture and a named source
— the Wall Street rout (S&P −3.7%, Nasdaq −5.28%, Nvidia −14% as the SEC
probes its accounts), the 6.8 quake in Peru, KL Rahul's 100 in Wellington,
Bitcoin sinking below $60,000, COP31 slipping into overtime with no deal,
Norris on pole in a wet Las Vegas qualifying, Trump's "very good talks" with
Putin, the missile strike on Kryvyi Rih, the plan to close Rikers Island by
2032, record foreign cash pouring into Indian shares, four nations demanding
Gaza aid access, Saudi intercepts over Riyadh, and Nepal's prime minister
heading to Delhi. A world map pins the 13 drawn stories, 27 numbered briefs
cover the rest of the day — gold at a 14-month low, the Palisades fire
verdict, a vendor cyberattack on the big banks, France's fourth weekend of
protests, and more — and a DATE CHECK box names the Friday and Thursday
stories left out (the Kyiv bridges, the Poland missiles, the ICJ Myanmar
ruling, the EU's €7.9bn payment to Kyiv, the Denmark plot reveal, the UK–EU
deals, the Anthropic debut). 40 stories in all, every one verified as having
happened, or first been reported, on 21 November 2026.

The image is drawn entirely in code: a hand-written canvas, a 5x7 bitmap
font, and 15 hand-drawn illustrations — a falling candlestick chart, a
shaking city, a batsman, a sinking coin, an empty COP31 hall, a wet-weather
race car, two flags at a table, a shelled building, a barred island, rising
bars, an aid truck in flood water, a missile intercept, and the Himalayas.
The program runs with no network and writes the same bytes on every run.

Run it with `mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts`, then check
`out/entry.png`: 1024×1024, 41,093 bytes, sha256
`654b2992960ffe7ce55bcb45fd271c0206b4f8f6b8f2e936f260436b49081d9f`,
identical across repeated runs.
